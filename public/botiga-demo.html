Truc Mallorquí — store demo

For the Lemon Squeezy review. Screenshots taken on a phone, in test mode (no real payment).

Truc Mallorquí (trucmallorqui.com) is a free online version of "truc", the traditional card game of Mallorca. Anyone can play for free against friends, other players or bots, with no ads. The store sells only optional cosmetic items to help support the game.

Pricing

One-time purchases, no subscriptions. Prices in euros, VAT included. Delivered instantly to the player's account.

€1.99Pack Festes de Mallorca

Seven card back designs inspired by traditional Mallorcan festivals.

€4.99Pack Fundador

An exclusive "Founder" card back and a star badge next to the player's name, for early supporters.

Purchase flow

1. The game

Truc Mallorquí is free to play in the browser or as an installable app. A "Botiga" (Store) button opens the store. Nothing in the store gives any advantage in the game.

2. The store

Two optional cosmetic packs with their prices (EUR, VAT included). Players must be signed in so the purchase is saved to their account.

3. Consent

Before buying, the player confirms they want the digital content delivered immediately and accepts the terms of sale. Then "Comprar" opens the Lemon Squeezy checkout (hosted by Lemon Squeezy; we never handle card details).

4. After payment

When Lemon Squeezy confirms the payment (webhook), the pack is unlocked instantly and the store shows "Ja és teu" (It's yours).

5. Item unlocked

The seven card backs of the Festes de Mallorca pack are now available in the player's appearance settings and are shown to other players at the table.
